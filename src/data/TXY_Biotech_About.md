# Zhuhai TXY Biotech Holding Co., Ltd. — About Us

---

## 1. Company Profile

Zhuhai TXY Biotech Holding Co., Ltd. is a **"National High-Tech Enterprise"**, **"Guangdong Yeast Derivative Products and Alternative Antibiotic Application Engineering Technology Research Center"**, **"Zhuhai Private Enterprise Innovation and Industrialization Demonstration Base"**, and a **Guangdong Province "Specialized and Unique" Enterprise**.

### International Certifications
- **ISO22000-2005 (HACCP)**
- **BRC** — European Supply Chain Quality Management
- **HALAL** — Halal Clean Food
- **Kosher** — Jewish Clean Food

### Brands
- **TXY**
- **Wanfukang**

### Operations
The company owns its fermentation base and has introduced German environmental treatment systems. It focuses on:
- Healthy food
- Nutritional seasoning
- Animal nutrition
- Biochemical culture media

### Product Range
| Product Category | Products |
|---|---|
| Yeast-based | Yeast Extract (powder & paste), Yeast Hydrolysate, Active Dry Yeast |
| Nutritional | Selenium Yeast, Yeast Protein Powder, Yeast Peptide Powder |
| Functional | Yeast Cell Wall, Mannan Oligosaccharide (MOS), Yeast β-Glucan |
| Culture Medium | Yeast Extract powder and paste for culture medium |

### Markets
Products are sold to **Europe, America, Southeast Asia**, and domestic markets.

### Wholly-Owned Subsidiaries
- Zhanjiang Wuzhou Biology Engineering Co., Ltd.
- Zhuhai Wanfukang Biotechnology Co., Ltd.
